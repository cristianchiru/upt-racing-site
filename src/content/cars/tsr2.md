TSR2 is the second car built by UPT Racing. Design began in 2016 with radical changes to both design language and technology. It carries the longest development span of any UPT car, spanning multiple generations of the team and the 2019–2021 pandemic period. It delivered the team's first significant international results and established the engineering platform that all subsequent cars would build upon.
