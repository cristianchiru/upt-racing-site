TSR3 EVO retains the TSR3 chassis and engine while delivering major upgrades across every other subsystem. Design took four months (September 2023 – January 2024), construction five months — nine months total. The result: 33 kg lighter than TSR3, more compact and efficient suspension geometry, an improved aerodynamic package and an optimised pedal assembly. It secured back-to-back P2 finishes at Formula Student Balkans.
