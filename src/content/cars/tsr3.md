TSR3 development began in 2022 with a full generational change in the team. Design took four months (September 2022 – January 2023), with construction completing in July 2023. Two major firsts: the team's first twin-cylinder engine (Yamaha MT-07) and the team's first complete aerodynamic package — front wing, rear wing and carbon fibre bodywork. It secured P2 at Formula Student Balkans 2023.
