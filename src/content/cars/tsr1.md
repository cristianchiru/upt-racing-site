TSR1 development began in 2013 alongside the founding of the team. The years 2013–2016 were spent forming the core student group, setting up the workshop, learning the Formula Student SAE regulations, designing and building the car, attracting sponsors and preparing for the first competition — Formula SAE Italy 2016. Refinements were introduced for the 2017 season, with the car competing at Formula SAE Italy and Formula Student Hungary.
