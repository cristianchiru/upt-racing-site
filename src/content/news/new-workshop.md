## A New Home for UPT Racing

After years of working in a cramped shared space, the team now has a dedicated 400 m² facility funded through a combination of university support and sponsor contributions.

## What's Inside

- **CNC Milling Machine** — donated by Kloss CNC
- **Full welding bay** with TIG, MIG and gas capability
- **Electronics bench** with oscilloscopes, soldering stations and CAN bus diagnostics
- **Design room** with 10 workstations running CATIA V5 and Ansys Fluent
- **Storage and logistics area** for competition shipping prep

This facility will allow us to manufacture components in-house that we previously outsourced — improving quality control and reducing lead times significantly.
