## A Historic Weekend in Piteşti

The 2024 Formula Student Romania competition proved to be our most successful domestic event to date. After three days of intense competition, UPT Racing crossed the finish line in **3rd place overall** — a result that reflects years of incremental improvement and the dedication of over 40 team members.

## Dynamic Events Breakdown

The endurance run was the highlight of the weekend. Our driver maintained consistent lap times throughout the 22 km course, finishing without a single mechanical issue — a first for the team in recent memory.

- **Acceleration**: 4.1 s (0–75 m)
- **Skidpad**: 5.23 s
- **Autocross**: 1:12.4
- **Endurance**: Completed — 3rd fastest time

## What's Next

With this momentum, we head into pre-season preparation for FSG 2025 and FS East 2025. The engineering team is already working on refinements to the aerodynamic package and a new ECU mapping strategy.
