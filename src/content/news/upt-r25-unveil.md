## Two Years in the Making

The TSR 4 Hybrid represents a significant step forward in every department. The chassis team redesigned the space frame geometry from scratch using FEA to reduce weight by 8 kg while improving torsional rigidity by 22%.

## Aerodynamics Upgrade

For the first time, we developed a full CFD-validated aero package using Ansys Fluent. The two-element front wing and revised diffuser generate 40% more downforce than the TSR 3 EVO at equivalent speeds.

## Key Specs

| Spec | TSR 3 EVO | TSR 4 Hybrid |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Weight | 210 kg | 195 kg |
| Power | 63 hp | 65 hp |
| Downforce | ~175 N | ~250 N |

## Rollout

The car completed its first shakedown run at the UPT test track on 8 March 2025 — all systems nominal, no major issues.
