Interactive Features

Details That Make the
Difference

GPU-Smooth Scroll Bar

A 3px progress indicator fixed at the top of every page. Powered by transform: scaleX() and a requestAnimationFrame lerp loop — 100% GPU-composited, zero layout reflow. Silky smooth at any framerate.

Dual-Ring Custom Cursor
Default
Hover
Click

Amber dot snaps to the pointer. Cyan ring lerps at 14%/frame — a physical spring feel. Colour-flips to amber on hover. Only on desktop (fine-pointer devices).

Animated Event Banner
LIVE EVENT
TSR4 EVO Launch — ARChA / UPT YouTube Live ↗ ✕

Shimmer sweep animation, pulsing red live dot, flickering CTA. Fixed above the header. Dismiss persists via localStorage. Pushes header and page content down automatically — zero content overlap.

Magnetic "Wake the Beast" Button
⚡

A persistent engine sound button on the homepage. On hover, the button physically follows the cursor with a spring bounce — 30% pull factor, snapping back with cubic-bezier overshoot on leave. Plays a real engine rev sample.

Why This Matters for the Team
Easy to Update

News articles are plain .md files. Any team member can add a post without touching code — just write Markdown, push to Git, site rebuilds automatically.

Sponsor-Ready

A fast, professional, mobile-perfect website is a tangible asset for sponsor pitches. First impression matters — slow or outdated sites cost the team credibility before the conversation starts.

Mobile-First

Every layout is tested at 375px and up. The mobile menu, engine button, stats and sponsor marquee are all carefully adapted — no pinch-to-zoom required.

Future-Proof

Astro's content collections make adding new cars, team members, results or news a structured, type-safe process. The codebase hands off cleanly to the next generation of the team.
