About UPT Racing

A Team Built on
Passion & Precision

UPT Racing is Politehnica Timișoara's Formula Student team — a fully student-run engineering organisation founded in 2013. Every year, our team designs, manufactures and races a single-seat Formula Student car at international competitions across Europe.

We operate across four departments: Mechanical Engineering, Electronics, Aerodynamics and Business & Marketing. Every member — from first-year students to senior engineers — contributes to the full product development cycle.

Formula Student is the world's largest engineering competition, run by the IMechE and SAE International. Teams are judged not only on lap times, but on design innovation, cost analysis, business planning and sustainability.

Why Supporting UPT Racing
Pays Off for the University

Student Development

UPT Racing is a living laboratory. Students work on real engineering challenges with real consequences — deadlines, budgets, safety regulations and international competition pressure that no classroom can replicate.

  • Full product lifecycle: design → simulation → manufacturing → testing → competition
  • Cross-disciplinary collaboration: mechanical, electrical, composites, electronics, business
  • Direct application of CAD (SolidWorks, Ansys), FEA and CFD at university level
  • Project management, budgeting and stakeholder communication skills
  • Accelerates graduate employability — alumni placed at Continental, Bosch, Dassault Systèmes

University Visibility

Every competition appearance is a branded opportunity for Politehnica Timișoara on an international stage, attracting prospective students, academic partners and industrial collaborators.

  • UPT name on car livery, helmets, race suits and pit equipment at European events
  • Media coverage at FS Balkans, FS Czech Republic, FS Alpe Adria and FS Italy
  • Online content: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn — active documentation of engineering work
  • Live launch event broadcast: TSR4 EVO reveal at ARChA / UPT Timișoara
  • Network of partner companies: Ansys, Haltech, Continental, Schaeffler, Hexagon, Motul…
